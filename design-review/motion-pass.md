# Site-wide motion pass

Adds motion to the existing design and content:

- Section headings reveal in staggered, masked lines.
- Gallery photographs uncover as they enter the viewport and move gently with scrolling.
- Gallery cards lift on hover and respond to taps.
- Hero and report values animate to their final amounts, with static accessible text.
- Report category and condition selections share smoothly moving highlights.
- Photo captions crossfade alongside product selection.
- Method and benefit dividers draw into view.
- Process photography slowly changes scale, with sequential evidence rows and an animated reference amount.
- FAQ answers expand and collapse with animated height; triggers expose expanded state and panel relationships.
- Closing photography moves with the viewport; footer groups reveal in sequence.
- A thin progress indicator responds to scrolling across the page.
- Navigation underlines and button highlights respond to interaction.

Motion uses the existing Framer Motion dependency. No backend, product pricing, or provider integrations change. Reduced-motion preferences suppress scroll effects, counters, and spatial transitions.
